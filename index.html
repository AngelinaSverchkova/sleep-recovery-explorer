About the data

What the study measured

Yang and colleagues followed 306 stroke survivors. They used ActiGraph measurements across three phases of recovery and assessed disability with the modified Rankin Scale at 12 months. A small group with long, increasingly fragmented, less efficient sleep had higher odds of poor outcome than the consistently good-sleep group.

What this simulation shows

48 fictional patients report whether they needed prompting or physical help to get dressed over eight weeks. The generator gives the group with fewer early interruptions a larger average improvement, with individual variation. The sleep thresholds, time span and dressing outcome are choices for this demonstration.

Reading the figures

The individual figure shows the percentage of completed morning reports marked independent each week. Bars underneath count overnight awakenings. Each cohort line averages the individual percentages with equal weight per patient. Groups always use week 1 sleep, even when you zoom in. Select a week in the individual chart to inspect a night.

The overnight view uses simulated Apple Health–style stage intervals. Estimated awake, REM, core and deep stages use fixed CET (UTC+1). Oslo watch exports are one hour ahead during summer time. Missing nights and missing diary entries are kept separate.
